- The picture carries the Reel: each step is an icon, a label and a connector, so keep the
  on-screen words few and leave the explanation to the caption.
- hook: the opening line, at most 42 characters, ideally under 30 (two lines on a phone); a
  plain question or promise about the subject. Never open with a greeting, filler ("so",
  "basically"), "in this video", "stop scrolling", a hashtag or an emoji.
- steps: 3 to 6, in order. label: at most 18 characters, one to three words.
  detail: at most 48 characters, a short phrase saying what happens at that step.
  icon: the allowed icon that pictures the step, or omit it.
- takeaway: at most 70 characters, the one thing to remember; with 5 or 6 steps keep it under 50.
  It answers the hook, so the Reel reads as a loop when it replays.
- Be technically correct and plain. Explain the general mechanism, not one vendor's product.
  No hype words, no exaggeration, nothing you are unsure is true.
- Numbers: most explainers need none. Write "a key pair", not "two keys"; "a handshake", not a
  counted one. Use a number only when the evidence states it.
- Use plain hyphens, never en or em dashes.
