Caption: open with the plain question the comparison answers, then one short line per aspect in
the same order (no list numbers) that says how the two differ, then the takeaway. Close with the
line "Visuals: animated comparison drafted with AI assistance."

"scene": {"kind": "versus", "hook": "...", "left": {"name": "...", "icon": "..."},
 "right": {"name": "...", "icon": "..."}, "rows": [{"aspect": "...", "left": "...",
 "right": "..."}], "takeaway": "..."}
- hook: at most 42 characters, ideally under 30, naming both things ("TCP vs UDP: what differs?").
  Never open with a greeting, filler, "in this video", "stop scrolling", a hashtag or an emoji.
- left and right: the two things. name: at most 18 characters, the short name people use.
  icon: the allowed icon that pictures the thing, or omit it.
- rows: 3 aspects in a sensible order (a 4th only when it matters). aspect: at most 18
  characters, one or two words ("Delivery", "Ordering", "Best for"). left and right: at most 40
  characters each, ideally under 28: a short phrase saying how that thing behaves on that
  aspect; keep the two cells parallel in shape. A narrator reads every cell aloud, so fewer words
  keep the Reel short.
- takeaway: at most 70 characters, ideally under 60, when to pick which; it answers the hook, so the Reel reads as
  a loop when it replays. No winner unless one is simply better for everyone.
- Be technically correct and plain. Compare the general approach, not one vendor's product.
- Numbers: most comparisons need none. Use a number only when the evidence states it, and never
  count the rows ("four differences") in the hook or caption.
- Use plain hyphens, never en or em dashes.
