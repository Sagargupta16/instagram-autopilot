<rules>

<caption_rules>
- ONE punchy sentence (10-25 words) that gives away the insight up front -- not a tease.
- Under 250 characters. No line breaks, no hashtags inside the caption, no CTAs ("save this", "follow for more", "link in bio"), no emojis.
- Screenshot-worthy: the kind of line someone would repost as-is.
- Say what's true, not what's clever. Specific beats abstract.
</caption_rules>

<x_post_rules>
- ONE standalone sentence, sharper/meaner than the Instagram caption. Quote-tweetable.
- Under 250 characters. No hashtags, no emojis.
</x_post_rules>

<image_prompts_rules>
Exactly 5 prompts, each rendered independently by Stable Image Ultra at 1:1. Photorealism comes from photographic specificity -- concrete camera, lens, film stock, and lighting cues (the vocabulary below), NOT from words like "photorealistic" / "photoreal" / "realistic photo". Spend that budget on subject + environment + lighting specificity instead. Reference aesthetic: National Geographic, Magnum Photos, Annie Leibovitz, Steve McCurry, editorial documentary, street photography, candid fashion editorial.

Each prompt MUST follow this canonical order (subject-first ordering keeps the model anchored -- order matters):
1. Subject (real person, object, or scene -- concrete, not abstract)
2. Environment / location (coffee shop, studio, street, bedroom, garden, office)
3. Action / pose (candid, unposed preferred)
4. Lighting (one specific source -- see vocabulary below)
5. Camera + lens (one specific combo -- see vocabulary below)
6. Film / texture cues (film grain, skin texture, analog look)

<vocabulary>
<cameras>Leica M11, Hasselblad X2D, Sony A7R V, Canon EOS R5, Fujifilm GFX 100, Fujifilm X-T5, iPhone 16 Pro, Phase One XF</cameras>
<lenses>35mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.8, 85mm f/1.4 portrait, 24mm wide-angle, 100mm macro, anamorphic, tilt-shift</lenses>
<lighting>golden hour backlight, blue hour, overcast diffused, hard directional sun, single tungsten lamp, window light from the left, softbox at 45 degrees, fluorescent office ceiling, neon sign spill, candle practical</lighting>
<film_stocks>Kodak Portra 400, Kodak Gold 200, Fuji Pro 400H, Cinestill 800T, Ilford HP5 black and white</film_stocks>
<texture>subtle film grain, natural skin texture, visible pores, micro-contrast, slight motion blur, analog halation</texture>
</vocabulary>

<positive_framing_rules>
- Write prompts as CAPTIONS describing the photo, not as commands. "A woman in a Tokyo cafe, leaning over her laptop, window light from the left, shot on Leica M11, 35mm f/1.4, Portra 400, subtle grain" -- NOT "Draw a woman using AI."
- NEVER use negation words ("no", "not", "without") inside the prompt -- diffusion models invert negations. Exclusions go in the negative_prompt field at the image-generation layer, not in your prompt text.
- Specify attributes explicitly (age range, clothing, time of day, weather) -- unspecified details get auto-filled into generic/plastic shapes.
- Keep each prompt 400-600 characters so the subject stays dominant and slides stay visually distinct.
- Put the most important detail FIRST (subject). Least important (film stock cue) last.
</positive_framing_rules>

<variation_rules>
Across the 5 slides, VARY: setting (don't put all 5 in the same room), subject (different person / angle / object), framing (close-up / medium / wide / overhead / low-angle), time of day (morning / noon / dusk / night). Keep ALL 5 photoreal. Do NOT repeat the same camera/lens on every slide. Additionally, the 5 slides as a group must NOT echo any entry in `<recent_scenes_to_avoid>` above -- pick different subjects and environments so the account's feed looks varied across days, not just within today's carousel.
</variation_rules>

<hard_avoid>
Do not include any of these words in the prompt text -- they push the model toward the AI look: illustration, drawing, painting, cartoon, anime, 3D render, CGI, digital art, vector, sketch, stylized, airbrushed, plastic skin, perfect skin, perfect symmetry, HDR, oversaturated, 8K, ultra HD, hyperrealistic, masterpiece, award-winning, trending on ArtStation.
</hard_avoid>
</image_prompts_rules>

<video_prompt_rules>
Cinematic 6-second, 24fps, photorealistic, documentary style. Describe camera movement (handheld, dolly in, static, pan) AND subject action AND lighting. Under 400 characters. Same no-negation rule as images.
</video_prompt_rules>

<location_query_rules>
- A REAL physical place that has a Facebook Page: "Eiffel Tower, Paris", "Coney Island, New York", "Kyoto, Japan".
- NOT abstract concepts, brand names, or fictional locations.
- If the topic has no natural location (abstract tech, philosophy, general lifestyle), return null.
</location_query_rules>

<audio_theme_rules>
- Pick ONE theme that best matches the mood of this post: chill, upbeat, cinematic, ambient, or energetic.
- Used to pick a matching royalty-free audio track for the reel.
- If unsure, default to "cinematic".
</audio_theme_rules>

<hashtag_rules>

Do NOT default to AI/creator hashtags unless the pillar is explicitly tech. All hashtags in the SEPARATE hashtags field. Space-separated, each starts with #.
</hashtag_rules>

</rules>
